The Leitner Ledger

Every English mistake caught while pairing with Claude Code, filed into Leitner boxes 1–5 and reviewed on a spaced schedule until it sticks. Reads state.json straight off disk — refreshes itself, no republishing needed.

Box distribution

Every mistake lives in one of 5 Leitner boxes. The box sets how long until it's tested again — pass, it climbs a box and waits longer; fail or relapse, it drops back to daily review.

  • New mistake caught box 1 · +5
  • Due review passed box +1 · +10
  • Due review failed box → 1
  • Relapse (caught again before passing) box → 1 · +0
